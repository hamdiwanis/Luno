Luno is a light, photo-first home screen for Android TV and Google TV. A new photo every ten minutes, your apps in a floating dock. About 16 MB of memory on Home, and just 12 MB while you watch something else.

Features

Everything a home screen needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

No rows of recommendations, no autoplaying previews, no account. Just your apps, a good photo, and everything below, all set from the remote.

Photo-first home

A new photo every ten minutes, in full colour, with your apps in a floating dock and every other app one press down.

Two homes

Switch to the Apps list: no photo, every app as a banner, the focused app named large and its colours washed behind. It uses about 13 MB.

Arranged from the remote

Hold OK on an app to move it, add it to the dock (up to six) or take it off. Saved on the TV.

Hide apps

Hide the apps you never open; bring them back any time from Features > Hidden apps.

Your order, or recent first

Keep the apps in the order you set, or let the ones you used last come first.

Updates you can see

An app updating from the Play Store, or anything else, shows "Updating" and its progress on its card.

Power-on app

Open your live TV app, or whatever you watched last, the moment the TV turns on.

Sleep timer

Turn the TV off in 15 minutes to 2 hours. Turning it off yourself cancels the timer.

Memory at a glance

See how much memory is in use, with a bar, and close what runs in the background in one press.

Background apps off

Switch Chromecast, screen mirroring or TV Guard off when you don't need them, and get their memory back.

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth

Your network's name without turning Location on, and the name of a connected Bluetooth device.

Stays light

Keeps Android's background-app limit set, and a script turns off the TV's preinstalled extras, all undoable.

Light by design

Built for a 2 GB TV.

Plain Java, no libraries, no Gradle. The photo is let go while you watch something, and nothing runs in the background. Measured on a TCL Google TV with Android 11.

Install

Three commands from a computer.

Luno isn't on the Play Store. Install it over the TV's own network debugging (Settings > System > Developer options).

Download the APK

From the latest release on GitHub. Android 9 or later.

Install it and make it your home

With adb, using your TV's address:

adb connect 192.168.1.20:5555
adb install luno-1.1.apk
adb shell cmd package set-home-activity --user 0 app.luno.home/.MainActivity

Turn off the stock home

Google TV has no home picker, so the stock launcher has to step aside. debloat.sh in the repo does it, along with the TV's promotions and preloaded apps, and can undo it all.

TV=192.168.1.20:5555 ./debloat.sh --dry-run   # see what would change
TV=192.168.1.20:5555 ./debloat.sh             # do it (--restore to undo)
